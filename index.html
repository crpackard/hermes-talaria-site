Hermes-Talaria

Hermes-Talaria is a personal assistant that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It is used by one person, its developer, and it is not offered to the public.

It connects to its owner's Google account so that the owner can ask it, in a chat, to read and manage their own email, calendar, contacts, tasks, and files.
